2. Overview

"Paint Path" is an offline puzzle game for Android. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No data is sent to the developer, to third parties, or to external servers. The app does not contain any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

3. Permissions

3.1 Vibration (VIBRATE)

The vibration feature is used for haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. on errors or level completion). No data is collected or stored in the process.

4. Local Data Storage

The app stores game progress and settings (e.g. volume, language) exclusively locally on the device in the app's own storage area. This data does not contain any personal information and is not shared with third parties. The data is automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled. You can also manually delete it in your Android settings under Apps → Paint Path → Storage → Clear data.

5. No Data Transmission

The app does not require an internet connection and does not transmit any data to external servers. There is no communication with servers of the developer or any third parties.

6. No Third-Party Services

The app does not use any analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase), advertising services, social media plugins, or any other third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

7. Android Auto-Backup

Android may automatically back up app data to your Google account if you have enabled this in your device settings. This is a feature of the Android operating system and is managed by Google. You can disable this in your Android settings under "Backup".

8. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 16 years of age and does not collect any personal data. Since no data is collected, there is no particular risk in terms of child protection.

9. Your Rights

Since the app does not collect, process, or store any personal data, the typical data subject rights under Art. 15–21 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection) generally do not apply.

If you nevertheless have any questions or concerns regarding data protection, you may contact the controller at any time: jjs-stuff-dev@protonmail.com

10. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The supervisory authority responsible for Bavaria is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

11. Legal Basis

Insofar as personal data were to be processed, the legal basis would be Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest) for the local storage of game settings. Since no personal data is actually collected, this information is provided as a precaution only.

12. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as needed. The current version can always be found on this page.
