2. Legal Basis

The processing of locally stored data (see Section 4) is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest). Our legitimate interest lies in providing the app's functionality.

3. Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. None of the following information is collected:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device IDs or advertising IDs
  • Usage statistics or analytics

4. Local Data Storage

The app stores only functional data locally on your device:

  • Viewed models: Which 3D figures you have already seen, so that new figures are displayed each time you open the app.

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

5. Network Access

The app does not require an internet connection and does not perform any network requests. All content (3D models, interpretations) is included within the app.

6. Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions (no access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, etc.).

7. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services such as:

  • Advertising or ad networks
  • Analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media SDKs

8. Android Auto-Backup

Android may automatically back up app data to your Google account if you have enabled this in your device settings. This is a feature of the Android operating system and is managed by Google. You can disable this in your Android settings under "Backup".

9. Your Rights

You generally have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Since the app does not collect or transmit any personal data, these rights are not applicable in practice. You can delete the locally stored settings at any time by clearing the app data in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

10. Changes

If there are material changes to this privacy policy, this page will be updated.
